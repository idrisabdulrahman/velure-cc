# Velure — Logo Guidelines (compact)

Master files: [`design/brand/kit/`](kit/) · generated by [`build_kit.py`](build_kit.py)
· concepts and history: [`concepts/`](concepts/) · task trail: [`../../prompts/velure-logo-design.md`](../../prompts/velure-logo-design.md)

## 1. The logo

- **Idea**: the initial **V** drawn as a spirit level — exact 60° arms crossed by a
  level line, with the form filled to that line in warm red. Precision made
  visible: the instrument reads the number, the number settles.
- **Versions**:
  | File | Use |
  | --- | --- |
  | `velure-lockup-horizontal.svg` | Primary. Nav, footer, documents, presentations. |
  | `velure-lockup-stacked.svg` | Square-ish slots: social avatar, signage, card front. |
  | `velure-symbol.svg` | Symbol only, when the name is already present. |
  | `velure-wordmark.svg` | Name only, when the symbol is already present. |
  | `velure-symbol-tile.svg` | App icon / avatar (ink tile). |
  | `velure-symbol-tile-small.svg` | Favicon and any rendering at 16–32 px. |
  | `*-mono.svg` | Single-colour print: engraving, fax, one-ink press. |
  | `*-reversed.svg` | On ink `#180F0A` or any dark surface. |
- Lockups are **cropped to the ink**, so a placed asset is exactly the logo —
  no canvas slack to compensate for. All three horizontal treatments share one
  content box, so colour, mono and reversed align pixel for pixel.
- Every master is outlined paths: no `<text>`, no rasters, no filters, no strokes.

## 2. Clear space

Keep a clear zone of **1 × x** on all sides, where **x = the thickness of the
V's arm** (40 units on the 256-unit symbol grid = **24 % of the symbol's
height**). The zone scales with the logo — never use a fixed distance.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal lockup | 140 px wide | 37 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 120 px wide | 32 mm wide |
| Wordmark | 120 px wide | 32 mm wide |
| Symbol, full colour | 16 px | 6 mm |
| Symbol, one-colour (`-mono`) | 24 px | 8 mm |
| Icon tile (`-tile`) | 16 px | — |

The one-colour symbol's level cut reads down to **24 px**; below that it closes
and the mark degrades to a plain V, which still reads. Below 32 px use
`velure-symbol-tile-small.svg`.

## 4. Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (uncoated approx.) | Pantone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm red — accent | `#EA373E` | 234, 55, 62 | 0, 76, 74, 8 | not matched |
| Ink — neutral | `#180F0A` | 24, 15, 10 | 0, 37, 58, 91 | not matched |
| Paper — ground | `#FCF8F5` | 252, 248, 245 | 0, 2, 3, 1 | not matched |

Values are locked in [`design.md`](../../design.md) as `--hm-accent`, `--hm-ink`
and `--hm-paper`. CMYK figures are conversions to confirm against a physical
swatch book before a print run; **no Pantone match has been made** — do not
order spot colour from these numbers.

**Approved pairs**

- full colour on paper / white
- `*-reversed` (paper + warm red) on ink `#180F0A` or any dark surface
- `*-mono` (ink) on paper / white
- one-colour light on ink: take the `-mono` master and set `#180F0A` to
  `#FCF8F5`. Not shipped as a separate file — no surface uses it today.

On photography, use the reversed or mono mark on a calm area, or place the tile
version in a container.

## 5. Typography

- Headlines and UI: **Inter** (400/500/600/700), loaded from `@fontsource/inter`.
- Numerals: **IBM Plex Sans Condensed**.
- The wordmark is **Inter Bold with −0.032 em tracking, outlined** — its `V` is
  redrawn on the brand's 60° angle with the level line, so the name carries the
  mark. Do not recreate it by typing "Velure".
- Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

## 6. Don'ts

Don't stretch or squash · don't recolour outside the palette · don't rotate ·
don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects · don't rearrange or resize
the parts of a lockup · don't place the colour or reversed mark on a busy
background without a container · don't recreate the wordmark by typing it ·
don't swap the level fill for another status colour (warm red is the single
functional accent; status colours are semantic and never brand).
